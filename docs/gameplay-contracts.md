# Gameplay contracts

These describe inspected implementation and regression suites. Known gaps are listed in [architecture](architecture.md#known-limitations) rather than treated as guaranteed rules.

## Local play and learning

The current single-player flows are learning and daily mode. Matching uses `shared/game.ts`: accents fold for guesses, Catalan Ç and Spanish Ñ retain their alphabet rules, punctuation/spaces reveal automatically. Repeated or invalid guesses do not advance errors. Terminal rounds ignore later guesses. Six incorrect letters lose a local round; local modes have no forgiveness.

Learning plays thematic units of exactly 12 curated words from `data/learning-units.json` (validated by `src/learning/units.ts`). Production shows only `ready` units; `draft` units appear in development and in local builds made with `VITE_LEARNING_DRAFTS=1`. Flow: unit list → word → vocabulary card → next word → unit summary. Progress lives in `localStorage` key `penjat-learning` (version 1, `src/learning/progress.ts`): per word only the latest result (`correct`/`failed`; a word is discovered once it has one), bookmarks, and at most one session per unit (ordered queue, position, guesses of the word in play, and the pending card). Finishing a word writes its result and the pending card in one write; a refresh shows the same card and never records it twice, and "Següent paraula" clears it and advances. Guesses are saved after every letter and replayed on return, so leaving learning is never guarded. Comença/Continua resumes the session or queues the undiscovered words in unit order; "Torna a jugar la unitat" queues all 12; "Repassa les fallades" queues the words whose latest result is `failed`. Completion is discovered ÷ unit words; every discovered word is either Encertada or Per repassar. Corrupt or foreign storage starts empty; IDs no longer in the dataset are dropped, and a session whose current word was removed moves to the next one.

The corpus CEFR metadata is no longer used by learning at runtime; `test:vocabulary` still guards it. Tests: `test:learning`, `test:vocabulary`.

## Multiplayer

Rooms accept 2–10 participants before starting, with 1, 3 or 5 voltes. (UI terms: a *torn*/*turno* is one setter's word; a *ronda*/*ronda* is a volta, every remaining player choosing a word once.) Only the host starts. Start snapshots the active roster, randomly chooses its first setter, and rotates through that roster for roster-size × voltes turns. Departed setters are skipped. Only the next eligible setter continues a completed turn. All eligible active players must request a rematch, which resets scores, rounds and readiness and selects a first setter again.

Each guesser has private letters, errors, status and timing. Setter cannot guess; setter sees the secret word and one observed board. Scores finalize once **all** guessers are solved/failed/eliminated, including forgiveness decisions. With G guessers, solved entries ordered at zero-based index i receive G−i points, other entries receive zero, setter receives zero. Round ordering: solved first, fewer errors, shorter resolution time, then original roster order. This intentional round tiebreaker must not be confused with total-match score ties. Match results order by total score then roster order; that stable order does not itself award additional points. Sidebar and final scores share competition ranking (1, 1, 3): equal totals receive equal places, with no invented winner. The sidebar ranks active members only and separately lists departed players (on stacked layouts it may be collapsed to a leaders/own-place summary, but the full list stays one control away); the final result retains the full historical match roster. Those populations can differ after departures. At match-over the results surface replaces the board and the sidebar: its outcome comes only from the top-score group of the `matchResult` snapshot (a unique leader wins, several leaders share first place, lower-place ties are not draws, a departed leader still wins), and a missing or incomplete snapshot shows “result unavailable” rather than inferred scores. See `test:match-results`.

At error six a guesser awaits setter forgiveness. Granting forgiveness resumes play with six errors and unchanged guesses; one additional wrong guess eliminates. Denial fails the word. Each guesser gets at most one grant per round. Pending requests are private to the setter and independently decided. See `test:game`, `test:n-player`, `test:match`, `test:multiplayer-ui`, `test:e2e`.

## Departure and reconnect

A seat is kept for the lifetime of the room; only an explicit leave gives it up. Players are **connected**, **reconnecting**, **away** (`active` with `connectionState: 'disconnected'`) or **departed** (`active: false`).

- **Reconnecting.** Socket loss keeps the player's participation and progress for `ABSENCE_GRACE_MS` (2 min, `GameManager`), counted from when the server notices the loss (after a silent network drop that can take up to Socket.IO's ping timeout). Returning within it continues normally, subject to whatever ordinary play happened meanwhile. The room is never paused: everyone else keeps guessing, deciding and chatting; only steps that need this player wait (their own guesses, a setter's word or forgiveness decision, a next setter's Continue, a reconnecting host's start, the last rematch confirmation).
- **Continue without them.** While a reconnecting player actually blocks progression, `blockingPlayerIds()` lists them: a lobby host; a setter choosing a word, or holding a pending forgiveness decision; guessers when no connected player can still act in the round; the next setter after a finished turn; and the last players a rematch waits for. The connected host (or, when the host is the one missing, the first connected member, who would become host) may continue without them, and so may the player who would take over a blocked setter turn. Each view carries `skippablePlayerIds` for its recipient, so the strip's "Continua sense … / Continuar sin …" action appears only then. `room:continue-without` applies the away handling at once and cancels the timer; the server re-checks permission, blocking and status: a player who has already reconnected wins (`player-reconnected`), and a repeated request for an already-away player is a no-op. Otherwise the away handling applies automatically when the grace period ends.
- **Away.** After the grace period the seat, identity, token, score and own round progress are kept and the player is shown as disconnected, but the game stops waiting, exactly as for a departure: the host role moves to the first connected member (or stays on the away host while nobody is connected, until someone returns); an away setter's unfinished turn is cancelled without points and the next present setter takes over (at round-over the turn advances); an away guesser is eliminated and their pending forgiveness requests cancelled. A guesser who goes away while the word is still being chosen instead sits that turn out. A guesser eliminated by absence, and a sitting-out player, are shown as waiting for the next turn (`spectating`), never as beaten. Away players stay in the roster but are skipped as setters; turns started while they are away list them as sitting out. Sitting-out entries are terminal, unranked and excluded from the guesser count G. Matches need two present (connected or reconnecting) players to start or continue; fewer finishes the match. Rematches need every present player ready, so an away seat never blocks one.
- **Returning.** The stored token resumes the same seat at any time while the room exists, connected again, with the current server state; nothing is undone. A player who returns before the word is set joins that turn; after it is set, they keep any earlier result or watch the rest of the turn as a spectator ("Has tornat a la partida · Mira aquest torn; jugaràs al següent") and play from the next. Returning never takes back a transferred host role, so there is only ever one host.
- **Departed.** Explicit leave applies the same game effects immediately and gives up the seat (resume is then rejected); a guesser who leaves during word selection stays eliminated when the word is set. Departed players retain historical scores and are separated in the sidebar. The last departure deletes the room at once.
- **Room lifetime.** A room lives while anyone is connected. When nobody is connected, it is deleted after `EMPTY_ROOM_TTL_MS` (30 min) unless someone returns first; away seats never extend that. A server restart loses every room.

Replacement socket identity supersedes the old mapping; the superseded socket leaves the room channel and receives `room:replaced`, and that tab stops resuming until the player chooses "Continue here". See `test:absence` (controlled time) and `test:reconnect`.

Completed rooms remain for rematches rather than clearing credentials automatically. Invitation previews expose only code, language, voltes, count and availability. Invalid tokens must not resume. Incoming invitations take precedence over unrelated stored sessions, including opener/duplicated-tab copies. Invalid invitations show their error rather than restoring another room. Credentials persist in local storage with no client-side expiry, so a refresh, a reopened tab or a discarded mobile tab resumes automatically without a name or code for as long as the room exists; a name and code alone never resume. A valid legacy session in session storage (the previous format) moves to local storage when local storage holds no valid session, and leaving clears both. Stored credentials remain until successful entry replaces them, explicit leave (including a confirmed exit while still restoring) clears them, or a genuine resume rejection (`room-not-found`, `resume-rejected`) invalidates them; timeouts and connection failures keep them and offer a retry. While a stored room is being restored, leaving through the header or Back/Forward asks first, like a waiting room. Same-room invitations still resume. Late resume acknowledgements/snapshots cannot override a different invitation. Match status takes precedence over retained round state; completed matches expose no word-entry/forgiveness actions or waiting-for-other-guessers notices. A solved player's waiting notice appears only while the round is guessing. See `test:lifecycle`, live `test:e2e`, `test:multiplayer-ui` and optional `test:browser`.

## Daily challenge

Epoch is 2026-08-16, timezone Europe/Madrid, ordered pool is `DAILY_CHALLENGE_WORD_IDS`. Day-index modulo pool length selects the same word on all clients with the same build/date. Do not reorder the pool or change IDs casually. The page reads the challenge at mount; automatic rollover while left open overnight is not guaranteed.

One local-storage record stores the current attempt, including guesses, completion, win and mistakes. Restore accepts only the matching date, filters/deduplicates valid guesses and replays them. Finished attempts remain finished after reload; a new date begins fresh. Sharing reports outcome/errors without revealing the word. No server, account or historical archive is involved. See `test:daily` and `test:vocabulary`.
